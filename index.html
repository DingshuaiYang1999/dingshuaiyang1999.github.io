About

I started in materials engineering, but my real curiosity has always been the machines themselves — and the question of what makes them intelligent. Watching humanoid robots learn to walk, manipulate, and reason is what pulled me into this field.

I believe humanoid robots and embodied intelligence are the future.

So I switched to mechatronics and moved to Erlangen — to learn the control loops, the manipulators, and the AI tools that will, sooner or later, walk through every kitchen and every factory floor.

Projects

Coursework and self-built experiments.

  1. MATLAB Laboratory Course

    Course lab · WS 2025/26

    Problem
    Content: Static truss, Dynamic Crane, Measurement Data Analysis, and truss structure optimisation. The lab used MATLAB for structural mechanics modeling, ODE simulation, FFT signal analysis, symbolic computation, and optimisation tasks.
    Approach
    Modelled a static truss and a dynamic crane, ran ODE simulations, analysed measurement data with FFT, and optimised a truss structure using symbolic and numerical methods.

    MATLAB · ODE Solver · FFT · Symbolic Math · Optimization

  2. Automatic Control I Lab / Regelungstechnik I

    Course lab · SS 2026

    Problem
    Content: lab stations include Two Tank System, MATLAB 1, MATLAB 2, Rotary Arm, Active Suspension, and Magnetic Levitation System. The lab applies fundamentals from Regelungstechnik A / dynamical systems and control to modeling, simulation, controller design, and experimental observation.
    Approach
    Applied PID tuning, state-space and feedforward control, Bode-plot analysis with gain and phase margins, and anti-windup for actuator saturation.
    Result
    Modelled, controlled, and experimentally validated real lab systems: a two-tank process, a rotary arm, an active suspension, and an open-loop unstable magnetic levitation system.

    MATLAB / Simulink · Controller Design · Two Tank · Rotary Arm · MagLev · Active Suspension
