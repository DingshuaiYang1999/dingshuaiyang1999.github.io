Learning German involved a series of examinations and certificates. I passed most of them; one attempt was unsuccessful.

The following is a short summary.

- I passed the Goethe-Institut exams A1, A2, B1 and B2 and received the certificate for each of them.
- I took the Goethe C1 exam once, in Munich, and failed every part.
- I passed telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule on my first attempt, with a moderate result.
- I passed the DSH with the overall result DSH-2.

## The Goethe Examinations: A1 to B2

The Goethe-Zertifikat examinations are organised by level. A1 is the first level, and each following level (A2, B1 and B2) builds on the previous one.

I passed all four.

The examination I describe in most detail is B2, because it was the last of the four that I took before moving to Germany. I took it at the Goethe-Institut Peking on 21 February 2024. The four modules are Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking, and each one is scored out of 100 points. My scores were 70 for Reading, 63 for Listening, 63 for Writing and 62 for Speaking.

A module counts as passed at 60 points, so my lowest score, in Speaking, was two points above the pass mark.

## The Unsuccessful Attempt: Goethe C1 in Munich

The next level is C1, and this is where my series of passes ended.

I took the Goethe C1 exam once, in Munich. I failed all parts. The certificate was not awarded.

## telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule: Passed on the First Try

I later took a different C1 examination, telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule, and passed it on my first attempt.

The exam date was 12 September 2024. I scored 163 out of 214 points in total, with 127 out of 166 in the written part and 36 out of 48 in the oral part. The grade on the certificate is Befriedigend, which is a moderate result.

It is not an outstanding result, but it is a pass at level C1.

## DSH-2 at FH Aachen

Four days later, on 16 September 2024, I took the DSH at FH Aachen. DSH stands for Deutsche Sprachprüfung für den Hochschulzugang, the German language examination for university admission.

I passed with the overall result DSH-2. According to the certificate, DSH-2 shows the language ability needed for unrestricted admission to all degree programmes at all universities. In the written part my results were 66.5, 90, 69 and 57 percent in the four sections, and in the oral part I got 67 percent. The certificate itself is dated 2 October 2024.

## What I Take From It

This account is not intended as a guide, since learners differ. The following general observations may nevertheless be useful.

- **A failed examination is information, not a final verdict.** The Goethe C1 attempt showed where I stood at that time. I later took another C1 examination and passed it.
- **Every level asks for more than the one before.** Passing one level does not guarantee the next one.
- **A pass is a pass.** A moderate grade is still a pass at that level, and my B2 scores were only just above the pass mark.
- **Read your certificate.** The scores tell you which skills are strong and which ones need work.
